SMART DISK — QUICK START

Requires macOS 26 or later. The universal download supports Apple silicon
and Intel Macs. Other downloads name the supported Mac type. No account,
subscription or developer tools are needed. Apple Intelligence is optional.

1. Double-click the ZIP to unpack it.
2. Drag SmartDisk.app into Applications.
3. Open Smart Disk from Applications.
4. Choose the folders you want it to read and allow access when macOS asks.
5. Create a smart folder, for example "invoices 2026 over 100 euro".

For Finder and Open dialogs, drag the Slim folder in your home folder to
Finder's sidebar. Keep the menu bar app running to update the drive.

FIRST OPEN — COMMUNITY BUILD

This copy is not notarized by Apple. macOS may block its first launch because
the developer cannot be verified. Only continue if you trust the person or
website that supplied this download.

After trying to open it, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll
to the message about SmartDisk, select Open Anyway, then confirm Open.
You may need your Mac's password. Do not disable Gatekeeper or your Mac's
other security settings. A managed Mac may require administrator approval.

YOUR FILES AND PRIVACY

The app reads the source folders you choose, plus its Inbox for files saved
onto the drive. OCR, indexing and search run locally. There is no telemetry
or account. Originals stay in place. Editing a file opened through the drive
edits that original. Removing a smart folder removes its search; removing a
file from a smart folder excludes it there. New files saved onto the drive
are moved to ~/Documents/Slim Inbox.

Recognised identity documents are hidden until enabled in Settings > Privacy.
Recognition is not perfect. Choose sources carefully before giving another
app access to the drive. The index contains extracted facts and, unless you
turn it off, document text. It is stored with owner-only permissions in
~/Library/Application Support/SmartDisk. It is not encrypted separately from
your Mac. Do not share that folder in bug reports.

TROUBLESHOOTING

No files yet: check Settings > Folders and access in System Settings > Privacy
& Security > Files & Folders. Large scans pause on battery by default; connect
power or change Pause while on battery in Settings > Folders.

Drive folder already exists: Smart Disk refuses unrelated nonempty folders.
Quit, rename your existing Slim folder to keep its contents, then reopen the
app. Never delete original files to resolve this.

App component missing: unpack a fresh copy. Move the whole SmartDisk.app
bundle, not a file from inside it, into Applications.

UNINSTALL

Quit Smart Disk and move the app to the Trash. You can separately delete
~/Library/Application Support/SmartDisk to remove its local index. Check for
files you saved before removing ~/Slim. Keep ~/Documents/Slim Inbox if it
contains your files.
